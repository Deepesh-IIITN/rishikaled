Origin & Mission

For over two decades, Rishika Enterprises has been the trusted manufacturer and industry leader for premium LED lighting solutions in India.

Our mission is to provide businesses and governments with highly efficient, eco-friendly lighting—ranging from massive stadium lights and highway luminaires to robust industrial lighting—designed to drastically reduce power consumption.

Built for Extreme Conditions

Every luminaire we manufacture is tested against extreme Indian conditions—heavy monsoon rains, scorching summer heat, and severe voltage fluctuations.

We use heavy-duty aluminum die-cast housings and IP66 rated weatherproof sealants so your lighting investment keeps shining year after year without failure.

Local Support & Warranty

Unlike imported unbranded lighting, we provide complete post-installation support and technical auditing right here in India, ensuring you meet optimal lux levels.
